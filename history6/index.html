Summary Of Class
  • We went over the Diamond RQ's! First you took a few minutes on your own to look back at your group's q's, then we went through them together while you took notes on the ones you didn't answer. The class notes are in the second photo.
  • Some big themes came out of it:
    • Agriculture brought progress (fire, then refrigerators, cars, machines) but also social inequality, with women made "beasts of burden."
    • A surplus of food led to more children.
    • Hunter-gatherers like the Kalahari Bushmen had more time to sleep and a more varied diet than early farmers.
    • There are trade-offs either way, though most of us wouldn't trade our lives for a Neanderthal's.
    • "I had an experience, therefore this is true" isn't quite a strong argument!
    • Overall, humans chose quantity over quality.
  • Then we talked about a new part of history class: Current Events Day (CED)!
