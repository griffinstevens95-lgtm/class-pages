Tuesday, 8/25/26

Summary Of Class

Today we started with your syllabus questions, you sent me some very good ones! And while we were at it, we went over how to write a 'professional' email: a greeting, the actual substance of the email, and a sign-off.

Then we tackled three terms: "history," "prehistory," and "big history." In the academic discipline sense, 'history' usually means human history we have written records for, and 'prehistory' means human history before writing. 'Big' history is the story of the whole planet, told without the kinds of sources historians usually use. It still counts as 'history' because it's a story told about the past, built from information we've gathered, in this case gathered through science!

We finished with a video that squeezes the whole big history of Earth into a single calendar year. We didn't get to the reading together (pgs. 4-9, also about the big history of the Earth), so that's first up next time.
