October 2026 History 6 Class Newsletter

Hello sixth graders and sixth-grade families! Here's our first class newsletter of the year. We've gotten off to a great start in history class, beginning with some clarifying discussions on the study of history and the work of historians. Research, discussion, critical thinking, reading primary and secondary sources, and written analysis is much of what professional historians do, and it's a lot of what we've been doing, too! We learned about the evolution of hominids in the context of the "big" history of the earth and the difference between "prehistory" and history, with early-human presentations and a comic strip project built from our Sapiens reading along the way. More recently we've been reading about the Neolithic Revolution, the beginnings of settled, agricultural, urbanized societies, and the sixth graders did a nice job on their first set of reading questions. All of this has led up to our first formal debate, happening right now: was the development of agriculture a good thing for humanity or, as Jared Diamond put it, "The Worst Mistake in the History of the Human Race"? Our first Current Events Day presentation of the year is coming up next week, with many more to come. On to ancient Egypt, in a couple of weeks!
