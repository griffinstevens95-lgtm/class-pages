Consuls
• Commanders-in-Chief

Peoples’ Assemblies
• Local gov. for common folk – civil law like personal injury, family law, etc.)
• Curiae that voted in blocs for magistrates (Consuls, aediles, praetors, etc.)

Pontifex Maximus – Highest priest, administered state religion, festivals, etc. Auguries

Censor – Keeps Census, keeps up "public morals"

Senate
• Controls treasury and the use of public funds (the budget)
• Senators served for life! Magistrates became senators once their term was up
• At first, only patricians could run for office!
